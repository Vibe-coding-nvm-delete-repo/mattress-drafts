Which mattress suits your sleep needs?

Why this matters to you: When a mattress exerts over 32 mmHg of interface pressure against your shoulders or hips, it exceeds the clinical Landis capillary closing limit. This compresses blood vessels, triggers arm numbness, and forces your body to toss and turn throughout the night.

The right Saatva mattress depends entirely on your body weight and sleep position. Your hips and shoulders carry about 60 percent of your total body weight. A good mattress must keep your spine flat and straight. It must also cushion your joints so pressure never cuts off your blood flow.
