How We Test

Same tools, every bed

Every bed is bought at full price and slept on for 30 nights across back, side, and stomach. We photograph spinal alignment, measure sink depth with a ruler, log surface heat across the night, and drop a fixed weight to grade motion control.

Scores come from the instruments, not from comfort alone. Failures, sags, and returns are printed next to the praise. Makers get no preview and no right of reply before publication.
